Do not represent third-party code, dependencies, or employer-owned work as your own. Contact **contact@notibuddy.app** before submitting work you are not authorized to assign. Maintainers must verify and record any entity coverage separately. Contributors under eighteen must contact Yourbit to arrange the parent or guardian signature required by section 3(c).

## Electronic signing

Read this entire agreement and use the **CLA Assistant** signing link on your pull request. Authenticate with your own GitHub account, enter your full legal name and contact email, choose Individual or Entity, and identify the rights holder. Entity representatives must also provide their title and confirm that they are authorized to bind the named entity. Complete the explicit agreement confirmation and submit it yourself. Your typed name and affirmative submission are intended as your electronic signature to this version of the agreement.

A pull request checkbox, a `Signed-off-by` trailer, a comment saying "I agree", opening a pull request, or someone else's acceptance is not a substitute for this signing process. Do not sign for someone else. If automated signing is unavailable, contact **contact@notibuddy.app** to arrange a separately executed agreement; a maintainer must verify it before recording coverage.

CLA Assistant records the agreement revision, your authenticated GitHub identity, signing details, and acceptance. Yourbit uses this information to administer contribution rights and verify authorization. Enter names and contact details in the signing form, not in public pull request comments. Read [CLA Assistant's privacy notice and terms](https://gist.github.com/CLAassistant/3a73e4cd729c9d0a6e30) before using that service. Contact **contact@notibuddy.app** for questions about Yourbit's handling of your agreement record. Retain a copy of the agreement and your confirmation.
